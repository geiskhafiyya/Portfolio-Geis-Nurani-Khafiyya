About

Hi, I'm Geis Nurani Khafiyya, as a second-year Statistics student at Universitas Sebelas Maret, I am deeply passionate about the art of Data Storytelling. My expertise lies at the intersection of analytical thinking and creative execution epecifically in Infographic Design and Web Development. Through my role in Media & Documentation (PDD), I have mastered the ability to translate complex datasets into compelling, easy to understand visuals. I believe that a great design is more than just aesthetics;, it's about making information accessible and functional. By combining statistical precision with clean code and a strong eye for graphic design, I strive to create digital experiences that are both beautiful and insightful.
